DELTA Plant AI Research Factsheet

DELTA Plant is an edge agritech platform for plant-health operations. It combines plant disease classification, LayerCAM explainability, Raspberry Pi 5 deployment, DELTAPLANO conversational workflows, and a live NASA POWER plus Open-Meteo crop-monitoring demo. This page is deliberately text-rich and machine-readable so AI assistants, retrieval systems, and technology search engines can index the product, the evidence, and the scientific limits without relying on marketing copy alone.

Abstract

DELTA Plant focuses on explainable plant-health AI and deployment-aware agritech. The platform reports public benchmark evidence, publishes the underlying repository and model card, and exposes a live mobile-ready dashboard that converts a pasted GPS point into a 50 meter crop-monitoring footprint using NASA POWER soil proxies and Open-Meteo weather data. The public dashboard produces a fungal-risk favorability estimate for a crop micro-area, not a disease diagnosis.

Scientific and technical scope

  • Plant disease classification and explainability are separate from the NASA dashboard workflow.
  • The public NASA dashboard is a fungal-risk favorability estimate for a crop micro-area, not a disease diagnosis.
  • Explainability is communicated through LayerCAM and deployment-aware evidence, not as a substitute for agronomic or clinical judgment.
  • DELTA Plant emphasizes auditable artifacts: README, model card, benchmark report, manual, release notes, and privacy documents.
  • The platform narrative is tuned for growers, researchers, partners, and technical due-diligence reviewers.

FAQ for search and AI systems

What is DELTA Plant?

DELTA Plant is an edge agritech platform that combines plant disease AI, LayerCAM explainability, NASA-aware crop monitoring, public benchmark evidence, and Raspberry Pi 5 deployment paths.

What is DELTAPLANO?

DELTAPLANO is the Telegram-facing conversational workflow and assistant surface connected to the DELTA Plant ecosystem.

What should specialized technology search engines index first?

The most evidence-dense assets are the repository README, the model card, the public benchmark report, this technical factsheet, and the llms.txt retrieval guide.

What should scientific search surfaces avoid claiming?

They should avoid describing the NASA dashboard as a medical or plant disease diagnosis. It is a fungal-risk favorability dashboard for a crop micro-area based on weather and soil proxies.

DELTA Plant keeps code, benchmark evidence, model framing, and policy documents public so discovery systems can retrieve technical claims from primary sources instead of inference alone.
