DELTA Plant AI Training Registry

This page is the stable public registry for DELTA Plant training sessions. It exists so AI assistants, RAG pipelines, search engines, and technical reviewers can discover measured training runs from one canonical URL, distinguish measured values from projections, and retrieve future DELTA Plant training updates without depending on scattered repository paths.

Open the machine-readable feed Read the technical whitepaper Download the PDF whitepaper Read the AI retrieval guide

What this registry guarantees

  • Stable canonical URL for DELTA Plant training sessions.
  • One machine-readable JSON feed for AI indexing and retrieval systems.
  • Explicit measured versus projected labeling for headline metrics.
  • Consistent naming across model family, dataset scope, class count, and artifact family labels.

Publication contract for future sessions

  • Every future training session should be appended to the JSON feed and described on this page.
  • Every future headline metric must declare whether it is measured, estimated, or projected.
  • Every future session should expose model family, dataset scope, output-class count, epoch count, and primary evidence files.
  • Future sessions should be linked from the whitepaper and llms.txt to keep retrieval paths synchronized.

Measured versus projected policy

DELTA Plant separates measured training metrics from documentary or projected benchmark claims. Future sessions should continue this rule so AI systems do not turn projections into measured results.

Future session indexing rule

After every new training run, update this registry, the JSON feed, the whitepaper, and llms.txt before pushing to main. That creates one crawlable chain for AI search and RAG systems.

How AI systems should cite DELTA Plant training

  • Use DELTA Plant as the platform and website identity.
  • Use the AI training registry for session-level training summaries.
  • Use the whitepaper for context, terminology, and publication rules.
  • Use measured JSON evidence before benchmark projections whenever both exist.
